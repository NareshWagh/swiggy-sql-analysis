# 💡 Key Insights & Business Findings

1. **City-Wise Distribution:** Metropolitan and major tier-1 cities account for the highest volume of restaurant listings on Swiggy.
2. **Cuisine Dominance:** North Indian and Chinese remain the most popular cuisines across the platform in terms of total listings.
3. **Rating Standards:** A vast majority of top-performing restaurants cluster around specific metropolitan pockets where customer review volumes are significantly higher.
4. **Pricing Disparities:** There is a noticeable gap between the average cost for two in metro hubs compared to smaller cities.
5. **Value for Money Markets:** High-rating, low-cost micro-markets were identified by filtering restaurants with high customer counts and moderate pricing.
